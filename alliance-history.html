How movement is calculated

Routes compare consecutive observations of the same player when a change of alliance tag was recorded and both tags are known. The move happened between those observations. Missing or conflicting observations are not counted as moves; a route can also reflect a tag change, not a transfer, because the map archive has no stable alliance IDs.

Each player counts once per origin-to-destination route in the selected period. With at least 10 player-route pairs and four distinct origin or destination tags, a gap of at least 15 percentage points between the top three destination and origin shares is described as concentration or spreading. This is an observation pattern, not proof of a merger or of activity across the full server. Periods use UTC and end on the day of the latest dated observation.
