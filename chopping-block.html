How the score works

Each category is ranked against current D3AD peers on the selected week (lifetime kills are the latest total regardless of week). Positive ties get their mean percentile; a recorded zero or absence from a captured VS/donation list is always 0/100. Weighted average: VS 40%, donations 35%, kills 25% by default. Lower is worse. A missing entire list, ambiguous historical alias or missing kill total is unknown, not zero. Scores with missing weighted data or uncertain identity, or a recorded join during/after the selected week, are provisional and are excluded from the bottom review group. If the weekly VS list exists, we use it once; otherwise we sum available daily lists and warn about partial coverage. Historical weeks use today's observed roster, so verify tenure; join logs do not prove uninterrupted membership. Frenzy points and outgoing attack events are displayed only, not scored, because those feeds can be partial. Attack counts are not kills. Power is context only.
