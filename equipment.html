Source and limitations

This uses the publicly accessible JeffxLabs Z-Route equipment dataset as a live external source, rather than republishing its full contents. The source models client equipment strengthening and promotion. This version compares strengthening and Mythic star-promotion stages. Their ability and stat benefits are modeled separately; a final in-battle combination formula is not verified. Manufacturing prerequisites and actual combat damage calculations are not included.

Equipment quality caps are Premium 0, Good 15, SSR 30 and Mythic 40. Costs and bonuses may change in future client versions. If the source cannot be loaded, the page reports an error rather than inventing values.

Independent Z-Route analytics tool · Equipment model attribution: JeffxLabs / ZR:Reference · Score values are user-weighted estimates, not official battle power.
