    03 / SOURCES AND LIMITS

    Recommendations vs. game rules

    Role priorities were adapted from community skill sheets. They are recommendations, not a single build or a promise that every allocation fits your budget. The Engineer sheet alone asks for 62 ranks.

    The client-derived Season 1 reference documents a class cap of 40, so the sheets’ level 45 and 50 entries are omitted. The reference also lists Cooperative Healing at +10% to +50% across five ranks; the community sheet lists only +10% to +30% in its description. If your live Class Hall differs, use what your game shows.
