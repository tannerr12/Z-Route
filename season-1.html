02 / Roles

Engineer or War Leader?

Choose the class that matches your main assignment.

Engineer

Good for regular research, production and ally support. At class Lv.15, rank 1 of Instant Research shortens each active research queue by four hours. Fill both queues before using it.

War Leader

Good for frontline combat, regular rally leads and troop recovery. Check live prerequisites before committing points to your combat build.

The client reference lists a free class-reset item at class Lv.10. Use a skill reset instead if you only want to rebuild the same class.

Other Engineer activations and their effects

Cooperative Research and Cooperative Construction (Lv.30): +10% or +20% speed to you and one ally for one hour, non-stacking. Cooldown: 23.5 hours.

Cooperative Healing (Lv.40): +10% to +50% Hospital capacity for you and one ally for four hours. Friendship Shield (Lv.40): 1, 2 or 4 hours of shield for you or one ally. Both are non-stacking and have a 71.5-hour cooldown.

When to commit or switch routes

Go: The path is connected, the adjacent stronghold is owned, the declaration window is open, and eligible marches and recovery coverage are ready. Switch or wait: A link is blocked, the target is protected, or coverage is short. Post the new marker before relocating.

The client guide lists Wednesday and Saturday city declarations. Confirm the live R4/R5 declaration screen for your target; neutral and occupied targets can differ.

04 / Campaign

Beyond the first city

Every reset

Collect seasonal production, raise resistance, check seasonal quests and match your daily stamina spend to unlocked rewards. Before a fight, check hospital and Emergency Center room. Recon Aircraft can help infected allies; prioritize the rally and garrison leads.

Territory and cross-Warzone expansion

Keep the route you already own defensible. A captured Lv.1 to Lv.5 city adds one to the stronghold limit in the documented rules. Raid the Radiated Zone outposts are event objectives. The later Alliance Outpost opens cross-Warzone expansion and cannot be moved once placed, so confirm the nearby stronghold and garrison plan before placing it.

Milestones, loot and settlement

Use the in-game panel for Expedition, hero and weapon unlocks. City loot is allocated separately from contribution ranking; have an officer verify distributions. Claim available rewards before settlement closes. Seasonal buildings go away, and class level and Class EXP carry over under the documented rules.
